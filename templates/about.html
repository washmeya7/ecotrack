What is EcoTrack?

The goal is simple: understand your impact, improve your habits, and create a greener future.

What is the Eco Score?

The Eco Score is a simple score from 0 to 100 that represents your environmental performance.

Your score considers different areas of your lifestyle, including energy, water, transportation and waste.

A higher score means your current habits are generally more environmentally friendly.

🌍

What is a Carbon Footprint?

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gas emissions produced directly or indirectly by our everyday activities. It is usually measured in terms of carbon dioxide equivalent (CO₂e).

Activities such as using electricity, travelling by car, taking flights, consuming products and producing waste can contribute to your carbon footprint.

EcoTrack helps you understand these impacts by using information about your lifestyle and presenting your environmental impact in a simple and easy-to-understand way.
